1 / 7 — PROBLEM STATEMENT HACKDAY 1.0 • TECH FOR A BETTER TOMORROW

The Civic Maintenance Paradox

Buried in Complaints, Blind to Real-World Urgency

🚨 The Complaint Deluge

High-density zones (campuses, townships, cities) generate dozens of duplicate reports for a single defect, crippling operator helpdesks and masking new issues.

📝 Unstructured Noise

Reports arrive as informal text, voice notes, and grainy images without standard categorization, physical GPS coordinates, or severity assessment.

⏱️ Timestamp Queueing

Tickets are sorted first-come, first-served rather than by dynamic urgency. High-hazard defects (broken streetlights on night pathways) wait behind minor cosmetic issues.

👻 Ghost Resolutions

Work tickets are routinely closed with a single checkbox click and zero photographic evidence, breeding deep citizen cynicism and repeat complaints.

2 / 7 — PROPOSED SOLUTION PATCHPULSE PLATFORM

PATCHPULSE: AI Civic Intelligence

"Detect problems before complaints become crises."

📡 Multimodal Signal Perception

Ingests casual citizen photos, voice audio, text notes, and GPS without forcing users through complex municipal forms.

🧬 Corroborative Issue Clustering

Fuses scattershot reports into single, deduplicated Issue Fingerprints using semantic embeddings, spatial Haversine distance, and temporal decay.

⚖️ Transparent Dynamic Priority (0–100)

Calculates urgency across 6 explainable factors: Severity, Population Impact, Persistence, AI Confidence, Night Vulnerability, and Risk Multipliers.

📸 Dual-Frame AI Resolution Verification

Mandates Before vs. After photographic proof, using computer vision to confirm physical defect repair before closing tickets.
