## 30-Second Elevator Pitch

We treat photos, casual notes, voice recordings, location, and telemetry as weak signals. Our AI fuses those signals into a single unified Issue Fingerprint, calculates an explainable priority score, automatically dispatches repair work orders, and verifies the resolution using dual-frame computer vision. We don't just count complaints — we reconstruct and verify problems."

## 1-Minute Pitch
"Imagine a student walks by a broken streetlight and snaps a photo. Minutes later, another resident types that an alley is pitch black. Then a voice complaint arrives. In traditional civic systems, that creates three separate tickets, three dispatch notifications, and zero context on urgency.

With PATCHPULSE, our PULSE-5 engine instantly perceives these scattered weak signals. Using multi-dimensional clustering across semantic embeddings, spatial Haversine distance, and temporal decay, it unifies them into a single Issue Fingerprint — eliminating 75% of complaint clutter. 

Next, our Explainable Priority Engine ranks the hazard on a transparent 0 to 100 scale, factoring in nighttime student vulnerability, 5-day persistence, and multi-source corroboration. It automatically generates an actionable repair order with recommended tools. Finally, when the repair is finished, the technician uploads an after-photo. Our computer vision model verifies that the luminaire is illuminated and the hazard cured before marking it resolved.

PATCHPULSE turns passive reporting into proactive, evidence-verified civic intelligence."

## 2-Minute Comprehensive Explanation
"Across university campuses, residential townships, and municipal corporations, civic maintenance suffers from three fatal bottlenecks:
1. **Duplicate Clutter:** When an infrastructure defect occurs in a busy area, dozens of identical complaints flood the portal, overwhelming operators.
2. **Black-Box Prioritization:** Tickets are handled either first-come-first-served or based on subjective opinions rather than empirical urgency.
3. **Ghost Resolutions:** Field staff mark tickets resolved with a simple click, while the pothole remains unpatched or the streetlight broken.

PATCHPULSE solves all three through our **PULSE-5 methodology**:
- **P — Perceive:** We ingest multi-modal weak signals from photos, unstructured natural language, audio voice recordings, and telemetry.
- **U — Unify:** Our clustering engine runs a composite formula incorporating Cosine semantic similarity, spatial proximity, and temporal decay. If three signals describe the same defect within 150 meters, they merge into one Issue Fingerprint without duplicate noise.
- **L — Learn:** The system understands context: is this near a dormitory? Is it dark? How long has it persisted?
- **S — Score:** We calculate an explainable priority score from 0 to 100. We don't hide the math — operators see exactly which factors drove the score, from nighttime vulnerability to 5-day persistence.
- **E — Evidence:** No issue is ever marked resolved without photographic proof. Our vision model executes dual-frame visual change detection, verifying a minimum 85% resolution confidence before closing the work order.

Today, PATCHPULSE demonstrates complete operational readiness on campus infrastructure. Tomorrow, this exact architecture scales to citywide civic governance."
